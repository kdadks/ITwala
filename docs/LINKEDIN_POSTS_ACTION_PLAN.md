## 📱 Quick Tips for Maximum Impact

### DO:
✅ **Post at optimal times**: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM or 12-1 PM (your timezone)
✅ **Use 3-5 relevant hashtags** per post
✅ **Engage within first hour** of posting
✅ **Respond to all comments** within 24 hours
✅ **Include visuals** (images, infographics, videos)
✅ **Vary link placement** (beginning, middle, end of post)
✅ **Test different CTAs** ("Learn more", "Enroll now", "Explore courses")

### DON'T:
❌ Post links without context or value
❌ Use only promotional content
❌ Ignore comments on posts with links
❌ Use shortened URLs (bit.ly) - always use full URLs
❌ Post the same content repeatedly
❌ Over-use hashtags (max 5)
❌ Forget to engage with your network
